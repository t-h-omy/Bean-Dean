🌱 Garden Bean

How to Play

🎯 Objective

Navigate the garden grid, rescue harvest fields before they decay, and defeat enemies using your card deck!

🎮 Controls

Desktop: Use Arrow Keys or WASD to move

Mobile: Tap adjacent tiles or use the D-pad to move

⏰ Time System

Each move costs 2-10 ticks. Harvest fields lose 1 HP per tick. Rescue them before they reach 0 HP!

⚔️ Combat

Draw 5 cards and use 3 energy per turn. Play attack and defense cards to defeat enemies and gain XP!

📊 Progression

Level up to gain talent points. Spend them on talents that improve your abilities and cards!
